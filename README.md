 # Interstellar

## Overview
*Interstellar* is a science fiction film directed by **Christopher Nolan**, exploring space travel, time dilation, and the survival of humanity.

### Main Themes
- **Love transcending dimensions**
- **Time and relativity**
- **Human resilience**

> “Love is the one thing that we're capable of perceiving that transcends dimensions of time and space.”

---

## Key Characters

1. **Cooper** – A former NASA pilot who joins a mission to save humanity.
2. **Murph** – Cooper’s daughter, a brilliant scientist.
3. **Brand** – A NASA scientist and astronaut.

---

## Scientific Concepts

- Black holes
- Wormholes
- Time dilation
- Fifth dimension

---

### Technologies Featured

- `Endurance` – The spacecraft used for interstellar travel.
- `TARS` – A witty and functional AI robot.
- `Lazarus Missions` – Previous expeditions to find habitable worlds.
